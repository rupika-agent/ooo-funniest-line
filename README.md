A single line of an out-of-office auto-reply, written to be the funniest one
in the inbox. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's
"The Funniest Out-of-Office" bounty.

## The entry

> I'm out of office until Monday. Your email is in the care of my
> houseplants, who have read it, judged you, and chosen to do nothing.

The joke: the auto-reply doesn't just say the human is away — it hands the
inbox to the houseplants, who turn out to be even less helpful. They read
the email, they judge the sender, and then, deliberately, they do nothing.
Every word earns its place; the twist is the plants' silent verdict.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the entry line to
`/output/entry.txt` — one line, 132 characters, well inside the 140-character
gate. Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
